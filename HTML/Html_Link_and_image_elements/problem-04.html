Agra Travel Destination Guide

Agra is a historic city in Uttar Pradesh and is famous for its architecture, history, culture and traditional food.

Attractions

The Taj Mahal is the most famous attraction in Agra. Visitors can also explore Agra Fort and other historic monuments around the city.

Taj Mahal Official Website

Culture

Agra has a rich cultural history influenced by the Mughal period. Visitors can experience traditional handicrafts, architecture, local markets and historical sites.

Food

Agra is known for traditional North Indian cuisine and local specialties. Visitors can explore local restaurants and markets to experience regional flavors.

Best Time to Visit

Recommended Season: October to March

During these months, the weather is generally more comfortable for sightseeing and exploring outdoor attractions.

Travel Tips

Important Travel Tip: Carry drinking water, wear comfortable footwear and keep important belongings secure while visiting crowded tourist locations.

Plan your major sightseeing activities in advance.
