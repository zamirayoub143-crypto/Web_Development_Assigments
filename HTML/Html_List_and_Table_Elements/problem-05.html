Registration Process

  1. Choose your competition.
  2. Check the eligibility requirements.
  3. Fill out the registration form.
  4. Enter your student details.
  5. Pay the registration fee if applicable.
  6. Submit the registration form.
  7. Save your registration/reference number.

General Fest Rules

  • Participants must carry a valid college ID card.
  • Participants must report before the event begins.
  • Respect judges, coordinators and other participants.
  • Maintain cleanliness throughout the campus.
  • Follow the instructions given by event coordinators.
  • Use of unfair means may result in disqualification.
  • Participants must follow university safety rules.

Important Terms

Open Event
An event in which eligible participants from different groups can participate.
Team Event
An event in which participants compete as a group.
Individual Event
An event in which a participant competes individually.
Registration
The official process of enrolling for a fest competition.
Coordinator
A person responsible for managing a particular event.
Final Round
The last stage of a competition where finalists compete for the winning position.
