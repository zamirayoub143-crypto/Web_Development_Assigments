Healthcare Terms

Consultation
A discussion with a healthcare professional regarding a health concern.
Follow-up
A subsequent visit after an earlier consultation.
OPD
Outpatient Department where patients receive consultation without admission.
Appointment
A scheduled visit with a healthcare professional.
