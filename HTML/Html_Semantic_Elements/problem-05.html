Event Overview

The National Career & Education Fair 2026 brings together students, educational institutions, employers and career organizations under one platform.

Participants can explore career opportunities, internships, scholarships, higher education and skill-development programs.

Registration is open for students and job seekers.

Objectives

  • Connect students with organizations.
  • Provide career guidance.
  • Promote internship opportunities.
  • Introduce scholarship programs.
  • Provide higher education information.
  • Develop career awareness.

Preparation Guide

  • Prepare an updated resume.
  • Research participating organizations.
  • Prepare a short self-introduction.
  • Carry required academic information.
  • Dress appropriately for professional interactions.
  • Prepare questions for recruiters and institutions.

Attendee Journey

  1. Register for the event.
  2. Check the event schedule.
  3. Visit participating organizations.
  4. Attend career sessions.
  5. Talk to recruiters and counselors.
  6. Follow up after the event.

Preparation Notice

Bring a printed or digital copy of your resume and keep your academic information ready.

Information & Frequently Used Terms

Career Fair
An event where organizations and participants meet to discuss career opportunities.
Internship
A temporary learning and work experience with an organization.
Placement
A recruitment opportunity offered to eligible candidates.
Scholarship
Financial assistance provided for education.
Apprenticeship
A structured training opportunity combining learning and practical experience.
