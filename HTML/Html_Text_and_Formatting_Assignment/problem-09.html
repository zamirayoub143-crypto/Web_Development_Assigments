Student Terms & Rules

General Rules

Students must follow university rules and maintain respectful behavior on campus.

Students should carry the required academic materials and follow instructions given by teachers. These rules help maintain a productive learning environment.

Attendance

Students are expected to attend classes regularly. Students should inform the appropriate authority when absence is unavoidable.

Examination

Students must follow all examination instructions. Required identification or examination materials should be brought according to university instructions.

Examination deadlines and reporting times must be followed.

Assignment Submission

Assignments should be completed and submitted before the stated deadline.
Late submission may be subject to the rules of the concerned teacher or department.

Submission deadline: Follow the date announced by the instructor.

Discipline

Students must behave responsibly and respectfully toward teachers, staff, and other students.

Discipline helps create a safe and effective academic environment.

Important Notice

Always check official university instructions for the latest deadlines, examination rules, and submission requirements.
