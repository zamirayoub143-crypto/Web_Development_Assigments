Science Notes

Chemistry

Common chemical formulas include: H2O, CO2, O2, H2SO4, and NaCl.

Water is represented by H2O, while carbon dioxide is represented by CO2.

Chemical formula notation uses subscripts to show the number of atoms of an element in a molecule.

Physics

Area can be measured in m2, while volume can be measured in m3.

Scientific notation example: 3 × 108 m/s.

Speed describes how much distance is covered in a given amount of time.

Biology

Biology is the study of living organisms. Cells are the basic structural and functional units of life.

Photosynthesis is the process by which green plants use light energy to produce food.

Quick Revision

Use subscript for chemical formulas and superscript for powers and scientific notation.
